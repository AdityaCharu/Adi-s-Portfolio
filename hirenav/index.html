    Why HireNav

    Built to be trusted.

    Speed only matters if you can rely on the results. HireNav shows where every score comes from, tells candidates when they are talking to an AI and leaves every hiring decision to you.

    Every score shows its source.

    Each score links to the exact line in the candidate’s profile that supports it. If a profile doesn’t show a required skill, HireNav withholds the score and tells you what to ask instead of guessing.

    Upfront about being an AI.

    Every call opens with the voice agent saying it is an AI chatbot and naming the company it is calling from. Calls are not recorded; only the transcript is saved.
